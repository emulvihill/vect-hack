## 2026-11-02 — The icon is the hero glyph, drawn as the game draws it

**Decision:** The favicon, home-screen icon and link-preview card
(vect-hack-j725) all carry one mark: the `@` from `src/render/vectorFont.ts`
— a 0-loop with a diamond, not a typographic at-sign — as an isometric
extrusion: a gold (`#ffd000`) front face, a wall-blue (`#1030ff`) back face
offset in parallel, and three blue struts. `public/favicon.svg` is the source;
`scripts/build/render-icons.mjs` rasterises the PNGs by hand, and they are
committed. vecthack.com's favicon is the same file.

**Why:** The hero glyph is the one shape every roguelike player reads as
"you", and the game's own letterform keeps the icon honest about what the
product draws. Isometric offset rather than perspective because a 16 px tab
icon has no room for a vanishing point, and two faces with a gap between
them read as depth where a tilted outline reads as blur. Gold on blue is the
hero against the walls in the quadrascan skin, and the highest front/back
contrast of the pairs tried (green/amber, red/blue, gold/purple,
magenta/cyan, ice/blue). Rendered by Playwright and committed, not generated
at build, so `vite build` needs no browser and CI stays GPU-free.
